How to Create an Account on Navox: A Beginner’s Guide

Published on 2nd January, 2026

If you’re new to cryptocurrency, Navox is the best place to start. Creating an account on Navox is super easy and hassle free. It takes less than a minute!



What is Navox?

Navox is a decentralized cryptocurrency platform where users can buy, sell, swap and stake digital currencies such as Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), and Ripple (XRP).

Think of it as an online marketplace for cryptocurrencies. Before using Navox, you need to create an account and verify your email address.



Step 1: Visit the Navox Official Website

Start by visiting our official at https://www.navox.io

Always double-check the website address before entering personal information. Fake websites can look almost identical to legitimate platforms.



Step 2: Choose the Sign-Up Option

Look for the button labelled "Get started" or "Sign up". Clicking this option will take you to the Sign-up page, where you can fill in your information

This includes:

  • Your email address
  • A password for your account


Choose a strong, unique password that you do not use on other websites.



Step 3: Verify Your Email Address

You will receive a verification code in your inbox.

Open the message, retrieve the code, and enter it on the Verification page as instructed.

This step helps confirm that you have access to the information associated with your account.

If you don’t receive a code, check your SPAM or JUNK folder and confirm that you entered your email address correctly.



Step 4: Get Your Recovery Phrase

Once your account is created, you will get your 12 word recovery phrase. This can be used to restore your account.

Please ensure that you keep your recovery phrase safe and do not share it with anyone.



That's all! You have successfully created an account on Navox!
